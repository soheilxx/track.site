## Limits of the method

- One sample point per node (the centre of the first text rect, at most 8 px from its left edge): a text that leaves its background box is detected only when the sample point leaves it — the German pricing sublabel was caught, other overflows at other widths/locales would need a per-glyph check.
- Backgrounds from `url()` images are not composited (none of the 1 935 nodes had one under the sample point: `imageLayers` is empty everywhere).
- The dark theme was checked only for the dashboard nodes axe had listed (light-mode sweep); the marketing pages in dark mode were not swept.
